What is MemeStand?

MemeStand (memestand.com) is a Drudge-style daily meme report. Every morning it publishes three ranked lists: the top 10 memes today, the top 10 trending memes this week and the best memes of the month (last 30 days). Each day's rankings are saved in the daily meme report archive and published in an Atom feed.

How are memes ranked?

Each candidate meme gets a virality evidence score. Every meme listed links to a real, checked source post or entry.

SignalPoints
Reach of the best post or compilation (X impressions, or views cited by Know Your Meme)10 × log10(views); 1M = 60, 10M = 70
Only likes known10 × log10(likes × 10)
Reddit top-feed position (r/memes, r/dankmemes)#1 = 60-62, −2 per rank
Each additional platform with confirmed spread+5 (max +10)
Documented on Know Your Meme+5
Picked in a KYM Weekly Roundup or Meme of the Month poll+3
X trending news story about it+5
Know Your Meme page views ≥ 50,000+5

Windows: today = took off in the last ~36 hours; this week = active in the last 7 days; this month = active in the last 30 days.

Credits and takedowns

All memes belong to their creators. MemeStand shows small thumbnails that link to the original source. Takedown requests are honored.
