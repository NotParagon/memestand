What does the Black Cat Clapping meme mean?

A green-screen meme built on an AI-generated video of a black cat with human hands and a human smile, slowly clapping. People drop the clapping cat into their own clips and captions to sarcastically "applaud" someone, usually for doing something bad or embarrassing.

Where did the Black Cat Clapping meme come from?

Know Your Meme traces it to a September 25, 2026 Instagram comedy video by merkurianin in which he feeds his cat and the AI-generated cat smiles and claps at him. On September 28, TikToker @asian5767 posted a green-screen version captioned about a teacher taking their phone, and the clip spread as a reaction meme through October 2026; it finished fourth in KYM's October 2026 Meme of the Month poll.

How to use it

Example captions:

  • “Slow-clapping cat as a reaction to someone's questionable decision”
  • “"I answered correctly and bro started clapping"”
