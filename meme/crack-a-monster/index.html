What does the It's Not That Deep. Crack a Monster. meme mean?

A short cope / shut-it-down catchphrase from Monster Energy's own X account: 'it's not that deep. crack a monster.' People quote-tweet or stitch it whenever a discourse feels overcooked, using the brand's own voice as a one-liner to dismiss drama and suggest cracking open an energy drink instead.

Where did the It's Not That Deep. Crack a Monster. meme come from?

Posted by the official @MonsterEnergy account on November 4, 2026 (X status 2118012916586676345). The brand post itself went massively viral and spawned quote-tweet remakes treating the line as a general-purpose dismissal meme.
