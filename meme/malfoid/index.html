What does the Malfoid meme mean?

"Malfoid" is a fan-art trend that draws Harry Potter's Draco Malfoy as a woman, usually a smug rival teasing Harry. The name blends "Malfoy" with "foid," a derogatory slang term for women, though most of the trend is lighthearted fan art.

Where did the Malfoid meme come from?

Know Your Meme says gender-swapped Draco art has existed since at least 2011, and the name "Malfoid" first appeared in a January 2026 post on X. The trend took off after X user @redteneri posted Malfoid art on September 22, 2026; that post has passed 23 million views, per X's public counts. Many artists followed in late September and October.
