AI Rumpelstiltskin Tip Toein' in My Jordans meme: what it means and where it came from

Credit: image via Know Your Meme; original creators as documented there. © its owner; shown in small form for ranking and commentary. View original

What it means

An AI-generated clip, styled like a 1980s fantasy film, of Rumpelstiltskin dancing in a barn, set to Riff Raff's song "TIP TOE WING IN MY JAWWDINZ." People caption it with surreal situations (dreams after cheap melatonin, being asked how many beers you've had) or use AI tools to swap in other characters.

Many viewers assumed it came from a real old movie. Know Your Meme confirms the clip is AI-generated.

Origin

Per Know Your Meme, the original video was posted on Instagram on August 27, 2026 by the pages Stroinaya, Neuroferma and Anna Glinskaya. On September 29, TikToker @6aimbot9 turned it into a meme, and on October 1 X user @itsjusttimbro paired it with the Riff Raff song, a post Know Your Meme says reached 9.8 million views in five days. NBC News covered the meme on October 7, 2026.
