Ken Paxton Eats Salsa Made in New York City meme: what it means and where it came from

“Ken Paxton eats salsa made in New York City.”

What does the Ken Paxton Eats Salsa Made in New York City meme mean?

A joke, not a factual claim. Posters on X took turns pinning petty, made-up "offenses" on Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton, each quote-post topping the last: he leaves shopping carts in parking spots, puts raisins in the Halloween candy bowl, and "eats salsa made in New York City." That last line borrows from Pace Picante's 1990s TV ads, in which cowboys are outraged to learn a salsa was made in "New York City?!"

Where did the Ken Paxton Eats Salsa Made in New York City meme come from?

MemeStand traced the quote chain behind the salsa post back to an October 29, 2026 post by @AustinConserve praising Paxton ("Ken Paxton is the guy who shows up with his lawnmower when you break your leg") and contrasting him with James Talarico. Critics quote-posted it with their own "Ken Paxton is the guy who..." lines on October 30, and @leftcoastbabe's salsa line came on October 31. Other branches of the chain may exist.
