How this works

Status is the shape of each meme's momentum score over the last 7 saved daily snapshots (fewer if the archive is shorter). One score per Pacific day: the daily list's score when the meme is on it, otherwise the highest score on the other lists that day. A day with no row is a gap, not a zero. New means the only score in that window is on the latest day and the meme has no earlier snapshot. Rising means the latest score is up from the previous score in the window and is the high of the window. Peaking means the latest score is that high and did not rise from the previous score. Fading means the latest score is below the window high, or the meme is absent from the latest day after appearing earlier. Biggest risers and fallers compare the score on the first day of that window with the score on the latest day, and only when both days have a score. A meme that just arrived is New, not a riser. A meme that has left the lists is Fading, and is not listed as a faller. This page does not change how the top 10 is ranked.
